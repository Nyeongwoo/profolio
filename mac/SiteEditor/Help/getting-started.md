# Getting started with Profolio

Profolio is a Mac editor for portfolio websites. You edit, preview and publish in one window.

## The pieces
- **The project file** (`.portfolio`): a package, usually in iCloud Drive, holding your site's content, images, Lab pieces and backups. Open it with File ▸ Open… or by double-clicking it.
- **GitHub**: where your site's files live online. Profolio publishes straight to your GitHub repository.
- **Vercel**: builds the site from GitHub and serves it at your address. Every Publish triggers a new deploy.

## The window
- **Sidebar (left)**: Site pages, Projects, Originals, Blog, Site settings, and the Lab. Click a page to open it; expand it to see its sections.
- **Preview (middle)**: your site as visitors see it, from this Mac's copy. Click a section to select it. The toolbar switches Desktop / Tablet / Mobile and the language (EN / KO / ZH).
- **Inspector (right)**: the form for the selected page or section. Text applies when you press Return. Toggle it with the toolbar's sidebar button. The Assistant (⌘J) appears in the same place.

## Everyday flow
1. Open a page in the sidebar, select a section, change it in the inspector.
2. Watch the preview update.
3. Publish (⇧⌘P) when you're happy. The toolbar shows how many changes aren't published yet.

## Feedback
Help ▸ Send Feedback… writes an email to Profolio's developer in your mail app, with your Profolio and macOS versions and any files you attach (a screenshot or a Lab capture helps). You send it from your mail app.

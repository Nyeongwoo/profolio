# Vercel

Vercel builds your site from GitHub and serves it. You connect it once per site.

1. Make a free account at https://vercel.com/signup and choose "Continue with GitHub".
2. Add New… ▸ Project, pick your site's repository, keep the defaults (framework: Vite) and Deploy.
3. Vercel gives the site an address like your-site.vercel.app. Add your own domain in the project's Settings ▸ Domains if you have one.
4. In Profolio, View ▸ Change Live Site Address… sets the address that Open on Live Site uses.

From then on every Publish in Profolio starts a new deploy; it's live a minute or two later.

# Translations

The site can show Korean (KO) and Simplified Chinese (ZH) as well as English.

- Switch the language in the toolbar (EN / KO / ZH). The inspector then shows each English text with its translation to fill in, and the preview shows that language.
- Pages with untranslated text show an orange count in the sidebar.
- Site settings ▸ Translations lists everything, including interface text that doesn't belong to one page.
- Project names stay in English on purpose.
- Visitors are sent to their language automatically on their first visit; the footer's language switcher lets them choose.

# Editing pages

- **Pages and sections**: open a page in the sidebar; its sections are listed under it. Select a section in the sidebar or by clicking it in the preview.
- **Forms**: the inspector edits the selected page or section. Text applies on Return. Some section types still use the web form: Edit in Web Form (and Back to Native Form) switches.
- **Section menu** (right-click a section in the sidebar): Show/Hide on Site, Duplicate, Move Up/Down, Delete.
- **New items**: File ▸ New… or the + next to Projects, Originals and Blog. Right-click a category to rename or delete it, or to make a project in it.
- **Items**: right-click a project, original or post to rename it, hide it on the site, move it to another category, or delete it.
- **Images**: drop or choose images in the forms. Profolio stores them as WebP. Replaced and removed images go to the Trash.
- **Go to** (⌘K): jump to any page, section or piece of text.
- **Undo** (⌘Z) works in the forms.
- **Minimal mode** (Site settings ▸ Minimal mode): one switch that trims the site to the picked projects, About and Contacts, e.g. for job applications. Originals, Blog and Archive drop out unless kept there. Turning it off restores the full site.
- **Turning links off**: a card's Links ▸ Disable links, a linked line's text style (Aa) ▸ Disable link, or About ▸ Page ▸ Disable every link on this page. The link stays in the page and works again when switched back on. Links to projects Minimal mode leaves out are off on their own.
- **Hiding a category**: the eye next to it in All Projects (or All Originals) ▸ Categories hides its tab and leaves its items out of the Featured / All tab; they stay reachable by link.
- **Home's Originals**: Home ▸ Originals on Home ▸ Hide the Originals carousel hides it all; Originals cards ▸ Hide on Home hides one card.
- **Archive link**: All Projects ▸ Text ▸ Hide “See all work in the Archive” removes the link under the grid.
- **Icon order on a category page**: All Projects ▸ Order in each category.
- **Languages**: switch EN / KO / ZH in the toolbar to edit and preview translations (see translations).

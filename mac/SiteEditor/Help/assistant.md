# The Assistant

Assistant (⌘J, or the toolbar's sparkles button) opens a chat about your site in the inspector's place; press it again to go back to the form. Each page has its own conversation, so what you discussed on one page stays with that page. Ask it how to do something in Profolio, about your site's pages, or for ideas; it can see your site's structure and content, what you have selected, and the preview.

- It answers in the language you write in.
- It can open things for you: the GitHub Account window, Publish, a page.
- It can change your site when you ask: a page's texts and settings, translations (Korean, Chinese), new sections, new projects, originals, blog posts and categories, and publishing. Every change first shows in the chat as **Claude suggests** — what changes, before → after — and happens only when you press **Apply** (**Decline** leaves everything as it was). An applied page edit shows in the preview at once and undoes with ⌘Z in the page's form.
- It can't upload or replace images; use the page's form for those.
- It runs on Claude Code, with your own Claude account. Install it from https://claude.com/claude-code, then run `claude` once in Terminal and sign in. If its sign-in expires, the chat offers Sign In in Terminal.
- What you ask, and the parts of your site it reads to answer, are sent to Anthropic to produce the reply.
- For interactive pieces, use the Lab's chat (see lab).
